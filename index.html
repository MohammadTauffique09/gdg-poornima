    How a semester runs

    Learn something on Monday, ship something by the end of the month.

    1. 1

      Learn together

      Short, hands-on sessions led by core team members and guest speakers. Bring a laptop. You leave with working code, not slides.

    2. 2

      Build in small teams

      Form a team of three or four, pick a problem from campus life, and build a first version. Mentors review your work along the way.

    3. 3

      Show what you made

      Demo your project to the community and push it to GitHub. Your repository becomes a line on your resume.

      Before you join

      Do I need to know how to code?

      No. The Web and Mobile tracks start from the basics. If you already build projects, join a team and help others or take on a harder problem.

      Which branches can join?

      Any student who wants to build with technology. Projects often need design, writing and research as much as code.

      What do I need to bring?

      A laptop and a free GitHub account. Tools and accounts we use for each session are shared in the group beforehand.

      Is this an official Google programme?

      Google Developer Groups on Campus is a Google programme run by students at universities worldwide. This website is a student-built project for the Poornima University chapter.
